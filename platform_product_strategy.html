The 6 things beyond information your platform must have

Show users their biological age trend line. A graph that moves downward (getting younger) is more motivating than any advice. Bryan Johnson's entire protocol works because people see the numbers change. Give users their own numbers.

A 10am WhatsApp or push notification that says "You had rice for lunch yesterday and walked after. Your 7-day glucose trend improved. Keep it up today?" is worth more than 100 pages of information. Timing is everything.

Duolingo built a billion-dollar business on streaks. Your platform does the same for health. "7-day post-meal walk streak." "30-day amla streak." Small wins compound. This is neuroscience — dopamine loops make habits stick.

People don't change habits alone. They change when they become part of a group that has a shared identity. "We are South Asians who are reversing metabolic aging." Anonymous peer groups by city, age, and health profile. Weekly group challenges. This is what no information platform has.
🩺 Doctor bridge — the missing layer everywhere
Your platform generates a one-page summary the user can show their doctor: "Here's my 3-month biomarker trend, here's what I've changed, here are 3 questions I want to ask you." Doctors love informed patients. This makes your platform sticky — it improves real clinical outcomes.
