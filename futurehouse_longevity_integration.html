FutureHouse — a nonprofit backed by Eric Schmidt, built exactly for what you need
FutureHouse is a nonprofit that launched in May 2025 with AI agents that outperform PhD-level researchers at reading and synthesizing science papers. Their mission: make scientific discovery free and fast for everyone. Sound familiar? It's your mission too. And their platform API is free to use.

The 5 agents — what each one does for your longevity platform
🐦
Crow
Quick science answer
Searches 38M+ PubMed papers and gives concise, cited answers. Faster than any Google search. Outperforms PhD researchers at finding precise facts.
Your useUser asks "Does amla reduce HbA1c?" → Crow finds the exact papers and gives a cited answer in seconds. Powers your AI coach's Q&A.
🦅
Falcon
Deep literature review
Digests thousands of papers and produces a full thematic review. Like a systematic review done in minutes, not months. Has access to OpenTargets database.
Your use"What does all the research say about South Asian metabolic aging?" → Falcon produces a full report you can use as your app's knowledge base.
🦉
Owl
Gap finder / "Has Anyone?"
Tells you if something has been researched before. Finds gaps. Formerly called "Has Anyone" — the name says it all.
Your use"Has anyone studied walking after rice meals in South Asian populations?" → Owl finds what exists and what's still unknown. Helps you build the most evidence-based product.
🔥
Phoenix
Molecule & chemistry
Designs molecules, plans synthesis, does cheminformatics. For the supplement analyzer — analyzes how compounds interact at a molecular level.
Your use"How does curcumin interact with the mTOR pathway?" → Phoenix gives molecular-level analysis you can translate into supplement safety advice.
📊
Finch
Data analysis
Analyzes datasets, runs statistics, finds trends. Useful for interpreting user biomarker data across your whole platform.
Your useWhen you have 1000 users logging HbA1c — Finch identifies which habits correlate with the biggest improvements across your South Asian user base.
